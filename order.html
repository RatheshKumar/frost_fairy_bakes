Before you say hello

A few little questions.

Not sure where to begin? No problem. We can work it out together in chat.

Do I need to know my cake design already?

Not at all. Tell us the occasion or share a reference, a colour, a theme, or even a rough idea. We’ll talk it through.

How do I find out the price and size?

Send your enquiry and ask us about the sizes and price for your idea. We’ll confirm those details with you directly before anything is booked.

Can I order one of the cakes in the collection?

Yes — choose “Ask for details” on any cake card. That opens a WhatsApp message with that cake’s name already included.
